About

This is an advanced speech synthesizer using phoneme-based formant synthesis with coarticulation to create realistic human-like speech entirely in the browser.

Phoneme Recognition:

  • Diphthongs - "ow" (how), "oi" (boy), "ay" (say), "ou" (out)
  • Digraphs - "ee" (see), "oo" (food), "ai" (rain), "ea" (eat), "oa" (boat)
  • R-colored vowels - "er" (her), "ar" (car), "or" (for), "ur" (fur)
  • Consonant clusters - "th" (the), "sh" (ship), "ch" (chip), "ng" (sing)
  • Special patterns - "tion" (action), "sion" (vision), "ough" (rough)

Natural Speech Features:

  • Coarticulation - Sounds blend smoothly like in natural speech
  • Formant Transitions - Vowel formants shift gradually between phonemes
  • Vibrato - Subtle pitch variation mimics human vocal cords
  • Stress Patterns - First syllables of words are emphasized
  • Natural Timing - Variable durations based on phoneme context
  • Prosody - Questions rise in pitch, statements fall naturally
  • Consonant Variety - Each consonant has unique spectral characteristics
